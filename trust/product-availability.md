# Product Availability

Quandora separates public research tools from controlled real-money execution.

| Capability | Availability |
| --- | --- |
| Factor Mining and factor backtesting | Available to public users |
| Factor Cards and research artifacts | Available to public users |
| Strategy composition and backtesting | Available to public users |
| Strategy paper trading and monitoring | Available to public users |
| Live trading with real funds | Internal invitation only; not open to general public users |

## Public Research Workflow

Public users can test factor ideas, evaluate evidence, combine eligible factors
into strategies, backtest those strategies, and run them with simulated orders.

These research results describe historical or simulated behavior. They are not
promises of future returns and are not direct instructions to buy or sell.

## Invite-Only Live Trading

Live trading is available only through an internal invitation. Receiving access
requires a separate review and authorization process. A public account, Factor
Card, strategy backtest, or paper-trading result does not grant live-trading
access.

Invited live-trading access executes an approved user strategy within the
permissions and limits assigned to that account. It does not authorize Quandora
to make discretionary trading decisions.
