# Community And Sharing

Discover, favorite, or share Portfolios through Portfolio Building. Reading a
shared result does not transfer ownership or make every constituent reusable
in your own research.

## In The Web App

Open **Portfolio Center**:

| View | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| Markets → Community | Portfolio publications currently visible to you |
| Markets → Official | The official collection; it may show **Coming soon** |
| Mine | Your own Portfolios |
| Favorite | Publications you have starred |

Open a card to inspect the author, introduction, composition, and available
research. Use the star to add or remove a favorite. Preparing research remains
pending; an empty chart is not evidence of a zero-return result.

For your own Portfolio, use the sharing controls exposed in its detail view.
Check the audience, valid-through date, and current sharing state before
submitting. The web app may offer fewer controls than the Agent workflow below.

## Ask Your Agent

Discover without changing anything:

```text
Use Quandora Portfolio Building to find Community Portfolios relevant to my
research goal. Show the authors, available research, and current Simulation
eligibility. Do not favorite, copy, share, or start anything.
```

To favorite a selected publication, explicitly ask the Agent to favorite it.
For sharing, specify the exact owned Portfolio, audience, and expiry. For example:

```text
Share this exact Portfolio with the Quandora user I selected, through the date
I specified. Keep the Portfolio definition and backtests unchanged.
```

The Agent must resolve the recipient and date if either is missing. Public
publication and sharing with a specified user are different audiences; an
instruction to share with one person does not authorize publishing publicly.

## Sharing Commitments

Sharing requires a valid-through date, inclusive in UTC; there is no permanent
or default expiry. While a grant is active, public and direct-user sharing
cannot be mixed. Active grants cannot be revoked, shortened, removed, or
switched to another channel. An active grant can be extended to a later date;
an expired grant cannot be revived by extension.

Editing the sharing introduction does not change the Portfolio definition or
rerun research. Downlisting an owned Portfolio is also not a way to revoke an
active sharing commitment.

Retained shared research may remain readable after expiry. Current discovery
and trading eligibility follow the permissions returned for that publication
or share; do not infer trading permission from a visible result alone.

## Use Shared Research In Context

Strategy and Factor analysis reached from a publication must stay within that
publication's authorized context. A viewable result does not expose private
source or let the recipient copy owner-only objects into Mine to bypass access.

For an explicitly requested Simulation, use an eligible publication or share
in **your own account**. The Portfolio Trading Analysis workflow checks current
permission when starting or resuming. See [Simulation Accounts and Runs](paper-trading-tutorial.md).

## Done When

Discovery identifies a publication and its available evidence. A favorite
request reports its saved state. A sharing request reports the exact audience
and expiry. None of these actions implicitly creates another Portfolio or
backtest.
