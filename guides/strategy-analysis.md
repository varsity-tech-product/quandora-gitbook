# Analyze A Strategy

Evaluate the complete trading rule rather than extrapolating from individual
factor grades. Start with one completed Strategy backtest you own or can access
through an authorized Community Portfolio.

## In The Web App

1. Open **Research → Strategies** and select the intended result. For Community
   research, enter through the shared Portfolio's research view.
2. Check its factors, weights, direction, ranking, and effective test window.
3. Inspect performance, drawdown, exposure, turnover, fees, funding, and
   attribution wherever retained evidence is available.
4. Compare only results with compatible periods and assumptions. Read the
   scope label: **ALL includes IS** and is not pure out-of-sample evidence.

## Ask Your Agent

```text
Use Quandora Strategy Analysis on this exact run. Explain net performance,
drawdown, turnover and costs, exposure, and factor contribution where the saved
evidence supports it. Identify the main weakness and one controlled follow-up
experiment. Keep this analysis read-only.
```

For Community research, give the Portfolio publication you are viewing so the
Agent keeps the authorized access context. Visibility of a constituent Strategy
does not grant ownership or unrestricted access to its factors or source.

## Done When

The analysis identifies the run, separates observations from inference, and
states whether the result supports further testing. Missing chart or attribution
evidence stays unavailable. It must not invent a factor's contribution from its
standalone Sharpe or a screenshot of the combined return curve.

Choose a follow-up deliberately: revise through [Strategy Building](strategy-tutorial.md),
evaluate its role in a [Portfolio](portfolio-building.md), or retain the result
without further action. An analysis recommendation does not modify an existing
Strategy or start a Simulation.
